HOW TO INSTALL FADERHOUSE (Mac)

1. Double-click "Faderhouse-Installer.pkg".

2. If macOS says it "can't be opened because it is from an unidentified
   developer": close that message, then RIGHT-CLICK (or Control-click) the
   .pkg and choose "Open", then "Open" again.
   (This happens because this test build isn't signed with an Apple
   Developer certificate yet.)

3. Click through the installer. You can untick parts you don't want.

4. Restart your DAW or rescan plugins. Faderhouse and Faderhouse Strip
   appear as effects. Logic/GarageBand use the Audio Units; most other
   hosts (including Max Sample) use the VST3s.

5. The Faderhouse app is in Applications. Open Options > Audio/MIDI
   Settings to pick your audio interface and MIDI keyboard, then click the
   "input muted" banner to unmute the inputs.

Works on macOS 10.15 (Catalina) or newer, including macOS 11 Big Sur, on
Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.
