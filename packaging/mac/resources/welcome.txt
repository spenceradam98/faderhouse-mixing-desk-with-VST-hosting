This installs Faderhouse, a 16-channel mixing console:

  - Faderhouse VST3        -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  - Faderhouse Audio Unit  -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  - Faderhouse app         -> /Applications (hosts VST3 instruments and effects)
  - Faderhouse Strip       -> one channel as a per-track insert (VST3 + AU)

After installing, restart your DAW (or rescan plugins) and look for
"Faderhouse" under the effects from YourBrand.
