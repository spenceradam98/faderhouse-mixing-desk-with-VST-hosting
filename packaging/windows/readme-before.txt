Faderhouse - 16-channel mixing console

This installs:
  - Faderhouse.vst3 and "Faderhouse Strip.vst3" into
    C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 (the standard folder every host scans,
    including Max Sample)
  - The Faderhouse standalone app (optional). It uses your audio interface's
    inputs and can host VST3 instruments and effects on every channel.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then
"Run anyway". This appears because this test build is not code-signed yet.

After installing, open your DAW and rescan plugins if Faderhouse doesn't
show up. In FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins.
In the standalone app, choose your audio interface under Options.
